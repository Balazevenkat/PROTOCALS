What is a Protocol?

A set of rules that defines how two devices communicate — what to send, when to send, and how to interpret the data.

/////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////

Why protocols exist

Device A wants to talk to Device B

Without rules:
A sends: 10110011
B reads: ????????  — has no idea what it means

With rules (protocol):
Both agree → bit order, speed, start/stop signals
A sends: 10110011
B reads: 0xB3 = temperature 179  ✔
